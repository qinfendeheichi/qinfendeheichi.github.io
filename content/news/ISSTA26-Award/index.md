  Thrilled to share that our paper **"How Does Killing Surviving Mutants Help Detect Real Bugs
  with Assertion Generation? A Controlled Experiment"** — joint work with
  **Vijay Krishna Palepu** (Microsoft) and **Prof. James A. Jones** (UC Irvine) — received an
  **ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Award** at **ISSTA 2026** in **Oakland, California**.

  Huge thanks to the ISSTA program committee, our wonderful reviewers, and everyone who gave us
  feedback along the way. And a special thank-you to my collaborators and advisors — this is very
  much a shared win.

  The paper is available on the ACM DL ([doi:10.1145/3832232](https://doi.org/10.1145/3832232))
  and in the publications section of this site.
